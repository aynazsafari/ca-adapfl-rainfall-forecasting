Source and temporal contract¶

The main cohort has299 training,35 monitoring,71 validation and96 evaluation months per client; December boundary labels are embargoed. Fit NPZs contain training/development labels. Separate inference NPZs contain no evaluation labels. MAIN q80 is derived from local training rainfall. Both delay arms instead use a matched297-month training cohort and its own q80 for auxiliary detection; the common MAIN q80 is retained only for cross-model rainfall-tail diagnostics.

Independently reconstruct overall point metrics¶

The following calculation uses raw saved rainfall forecasts and equal-client averages. It does not import production evaluation code. Paired-bootstrap and annual-sign results were independently recomputed separately; their receipt records294000 checked draws.

Physical output and high-rainfall tradeoffs¶

Nonnegative decoding applies softplus in physical millimetres in training, checkpoint selection and inference. Central pooling preserves per-row client target mean/SD. Tail weighting doubles local TRAIN-q80 exceedance weights with a fixed whole-TRAIN normalization. Compare overall/nonheavy errors, signed tail bias and negative counts together. A monthly q80 exceedance is not a daily hydrological extreme. A common output-loss variant was chosen on validation across four methods and five seeds.

The36 newly declared contrasts (32 fresh-output and4 archive-based v2 controls) form a separate annual-sign/Holm family. With eight annual blocks, the minimum first adjusted p is0.28125; non-rejection cannot establish equivalence. Eleven archived baseline/router and two archived feature comparisons have unadjusted exploratory intervals.

Reproduce or inspect¶

See docs/REVISION_V3_REPRODUCTION.md. In a fresh source checkout, python tools/reproduce_revision_v3.py prepare authenticates inputs and numerical contracts; train --execute performs the fixed100-fit budget; evaluate --execute authenticates frozen choices before opening truth. In the delivered evidence checkout, verify writes a new timestamped independent verification receipt without changing accepted results. No public repository or DOI is claimed.
